Capy Amigo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Developer: Adnan Riaz · Contact: alutori@gmail.com

Capy Amigo is a virtual pet game made for children. We designed it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data, as required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and the App Store and Google Play rules for children's apps.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no birth dates, no photos, no location.
  • No chat and no way for a child to contact other people.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • We never sell or share children's personal information.

Data that stays on the device

  • Game progress (your pet, coins, outfits, settings) is saved only on the device.
  • Microphone: if the child chooses the "repeat my voice" game, the app records a few seconds of audio to play it back with a funny voice. The recording never leaves the device and is deleted right after it is played. The microphone is used only after permission is granted.

Purchases

Optional Premium subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see payment details. All purchases are placed behind a parental gate (a question only an adult can answer).

Advertising

  • iPhone and iPad: the app shows no advertising.
  • Android: the app may show ads from Google AdMob configured for children ("child-directed treatment", content rated G). These ads are non-personalized: no advertising ID is used for profiling, and Google processes limited technical data only as needed to deliver the ad, under its families policies.

Parents

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing stored on our side to review or delete. Uninstalling the app removes all data saved on the device. For any question, write to alutori@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Capy Amigo – Informativa sulla privacy

Capy Amigo è un gioco per bambini. Non raccoglie dati personali: niente account, nomi, email, foto, posizione, chat, statistiche o tracciamento. I progressi del gioco restano solo sul dispositivo. Il microfono, solo se il bambino usa il gioco "ripeti la mia voce", registra pochi secondi che non lasciano il dispositivo e vengono cancellati subito dopo. Gli abbonamenti Premium sono gestiti da Apple o Google e sono protetti da un blocco per adulti. Su iPhone e iPad non ci sono annunci; su Android possono esserci annunci AdMob per bambini, non personalizzati. Per domande: alutori@gmail.com.
