ChannelRise – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the ChannelRise app (Android package com.channelrise.app, also available on the Apple App Store) collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. The app is published by DreamcodeStudio ("we", "us").

Contact: alutori@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • You use the app with an anonymous account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • We store your app progress (coins, tasks, campaigns) in Google Firebase so it works across sessions.
  • On Android the app shows ads from Google AdMob (the iPhone version shows no ads). In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information. The app has no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and app data (Google Firebase)

When you open the app it signs you in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which creates a random user ID. With that ID we store, in Google Cloud Firestore:

  • Your coin balance, coins earned and spent, streak, tasks completed and videos watched/promoted, and the timestamps of account creation and last login.
  • The list of tasks you already completed (the ID of the video or channel and the task type), used to avoid paying the same task twice.
  • Wheel, ad-budget and ad-free timers, and whether you have an ad-free period or the lifetime ad-free purchase.
  • If you enter an invite code: the user ID of the person who invited you, used to pay the referral reward.
  • Campaigns you create: the public link or ID of the YouTube video or channel you promote, its title, thumbnail and channel name as published on YouTube, the type and number of actions requested (subscribers, views, comments or likes), how many have been received, the cost in coins, whether bought coins were used, the device type (Android or iPhone) and the campaign status. Campaign details are visible to other users of the app, because they are shown in the "earn" feed.
  • Delivery records (which user completed which campaign task), used so each user is counted only once per campaign, and task verification records used to detect fraudulent completions.
  • Purchase records: your user ID, the product purchased, the coins granted, the price and currency shown by the store, the store transaction ID, the device type and the time of purchase.

Firebase (Google) also processes technical data such as your IP address and device/app instance identifiers to operate authentication and the database.

2.2 Public social media data

To show videos and verify campaigns, the app sends public YouTube video and channel IDs and links:

  • to the YouTube Data API (Google) to search for videos and read public video and channel information (titles, thumbnails, view/subscriber counts); search words you type are sent to YouTube for this purpose;
  • to our own server operated by DreamcodeStudio, which reads public counters (for example subscriber or view counts) of the promoted content;
  • thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image servers.

These services receive your IP address as part of the normal internet request. We do not access your private social media accounts and we never ask for your social media passwords. When you perform a task, the app opens YouTube; what you do there is governed by YouTube's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2.3 Advertising data (Google AdMob)

On Android the app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. The iPhone version shows no ads. Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and your interactions with ads, to deliver, measure and (where you allow it) personalise ads and to prevent fraud. The app declares the AD_ID permission for this purpose. On iOS, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.4 Purchases (Google Play / Apple App Store)

Coin packs and the lifetime ad-free option are sold through Google Play Billing on Android and Apple In-App Purchase on iPhone. Google or Apple processes the payment; we only receive confirmation of which product was bought so we can credit it to your account.

2.5 Permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load content, ads and save your progress.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) – used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Billing – added by Google Play Billing for in-app purchases.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

3. Consent for ads (GDPR, EEA/UK/Switzerland)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show Google's consent message the first time you open it (and again when your choice needs to be renewed). Ads are not requested until this step is completed. You can:

  • accept personalised ads,
  • refuse or customise the purposes and the advertising partners (in that case Google may show non-personalised or limited ads, which still use cookies/identifiers for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement where permitted by law).

You can change your choice at any time from Profile → Privacy settings in the app.

Legal bases (GDPR): consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalised advertising and access to device identifiers; performance of the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b)) for your account, coins, campaigns and purchases; our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in preventing fraud and abuse of the reward system.

4. How we use information

  • To run the app: your anonymous account, coins, tasks, campaigns, referrals and purchases.
  • To verify that tasks were really completed and to prevent fraud.
  • To show ads and to grant ad-free periods you purchased.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

We do not use your information for automated decisions with legal effects, and we do not sell it.

5. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) – hosting of your account and app data.
  • Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform) – advertising and consent management.
  • Google (YouTube Data API) – public video and channel information.
  • Google Play and Apple App Store – purchases.
  • Other users of the app see the public campaign details described in section 2.1.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

6. Retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while you use the app. Signing out of the anonymous account creates a new empty account; the old data is not reachable from the app any more. To have your data deleted, email alutori@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my ChannelRise data" and, if possible, a screenshot of your Profile page. We delete or anonymise your account, coins, task records and campaigns, except purchase and anti-fraud records that we must keep for legal or accounting reasons. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (Profile → Privacy settings) without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). To exercise your rights, write to alutori@gmail.com.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA where local law requires parental consent). We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

9. Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase, which protects it in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit the data we collect to what the app needs.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published at the same address with a new "Last updated" date.
