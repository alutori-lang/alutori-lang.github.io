Privacy Policy – Doc Reminder (Document Reminder: Expiry Alarm)

Operator: DreamcodeStudio

Contact: salsostaff20@gmail.com

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short

Doc Reminder keeps your documents on your phone. There is no account and we run no server: we never receive your documents, your photos or the text read from them. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Who we are

Doc Reminder is developed and operated by DreamcodeStudio. For any privacy question, write to salsostaff20@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

Everything you add to the app is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • the documents and deadlines you add (type, name, expiry date, number, notes, renewal history);
  • the people and vehicles you create;
  • the photos of documents you take or choose;
  • your settings.

Reading documents. Photos are taken with your phone's own camera app or chosen with the system photo picker. The text is recognised on your device (Google ML Kit on-device text recognition). The photo and the recognised text are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. The temporary copy the camera or picker leaves in the app's cache is deleted when you save or cancel. Location and other camera metadata are removed from the photos the app stores (in the rare case that a photo cannot be processed it is kept exactly as the camera delivered it).

Device backups. The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your documents are not copied to a cloud account by the system. To recover from a damaged file, the app keeps one previous copy of its own data file in the same private storage on the phone.

Your own backups. You can export a backup file from Settings. It is encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256) and saved where you decide. We never receive this file or the password. If you forget the password, the backup cannot be opened. The app-lock setting and the Premium purchase are not taken from a backup file.

Reminders. Notifications are scheduled on your phone. No reminder data is sent anywhere. A reminder normally shows the document's name and the person it belongs to; while the app lock is on it shows neither.

App lock. If you turn it on, your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN check is used. The app never sees or stores your biometric data. While it is on, the app's content is hidden in the list of recent apps and, on Android, screenshots of the app are blocked.

Data processed by third parties

Advertising (free version only). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. A banner is shown under the Upcoming list (the list of your documents) and a full-screen ad may appear when you return to that list. Ads never appear on the scan, review, edit, document and photo screens. The app passes no information about your documents to the advertising service: not their names, not their dates, not their photos or text. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process the advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first (Google User Messaging Platform); in those places an "Advertising privacy choices" entry appears in Settings so you can change your choice later. On iPhone, tracking is used only if you allow it in the system prompt. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases. If you buy Premium, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive only the confirmation that the purchase is valid; we never see your payment details.

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to remind you before something expires.
  • Run at start-up – to restore scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Biometrics – for the optional app lock.
  • Internet / network state / advertising ID – for ads in the free version.
  • Billing – for the optional Premium purchase.

The app does not ask for the camera permission (photos are taken by your phone's camera app, which hands the picture to Doc Reminder), nor for access to your files, photo library, contacts or location.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a single document (with its photos and reminders), or everything at once with Settings › Delete all data, which removes every document, person, photo, temporary photo copy, scheduled reminder and setting, including the previous copy of the data file; only the fact that Premium was bought is remembered. Uninstalling the app also removes all its data. Data held by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Not legal advice

Doc Reminder is a reminder tool. Suggested dates and helper texts are for convenience and are not legal advice; always check your documents and the competent offices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an updated date.
