Easy Screen Recorder Pro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Easy Screen Recorder Pro app (shown on the device as "Screen Recorder", Android package com.dreamcodestudio.easyscreenrecorder) collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. The app is published by DreamcodeStudio ("we", "us").

Contact: salsostaff20@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • Your recordings stay on your device, including their sound. They are saved in the app's own folder on your phone and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • No account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Ads are never shown while you are recording, so they never appear in your videos.
  • We do not sell your personal information. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK and we run no server for it.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Screen recordings

When you start a recording, Android asks for your permission every time (the system screen-capture dialog). The video of your screen is written as an MP4 file in the app-specific folder on your device (Android/data/com.dreamcodestudio.easyscreenrecorder/files/recordings). It is processed only on your device. We never receive, see or upload your recordings. When you tap a recording, it is opened with the video player you choose on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes this folder and the recordings in it.

A screen recording can contain whatever is on your screen, including notifications and personal information. Please close notifications and hide sensitive data before recording, and be careful when you share a video with others.

2.2 Audio

Sound is recorded only if you turn it on. The Microphone switch records your voice through the microphone; the Device audio switch (Android 10 and later) records the sound played by apps and games, using Android's audio playback capture together with the screen-capture permission you give for that recording. Both switches need the Android microphone permission, which the app asks for only when you turn one of them on; if you refuse, recording works without sound. The sound is mixed into the MP4 file on your device, in the same app folder as the video, and is never uploaded, sent to us or shared. Some apps do not allow their sound to be recorded; Android then keeps them silent. While a recording with sound is running, Android shows its microphone indicator.

2.3 Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner and interstitial ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and your interactions with ads, to deliver, measure and (where you allow it) personalise ads and to prevent fraud. The app declares the AD_ID permission for this purpose.

See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.4 Permissions

  • Foreground service (media projection, and microphone when sound is on) – keeps the screen recording, and its sound if you turned it on, running while you use other apps, with a notification shown for the whole recording.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) – asked only when you turn on the Microphone or Device audio switch; used only during a recording you started, to put sound in your video on your device.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later) – shows the "Screen recording in progress" notification with its Stop button.
  • Internet / network state – used only by Google AdMob to load ads and by Google's consent message.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) – used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, photos, other files or location, and uses the microphone only as described above.

3. Consent for ads (GDPR, EEA/UK/Switzerland)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show Google's consent message when you open it for the first time (and again when your choice needs to be renewed). Ads are not requested until this step is completed. You can:

  • accept personalised ads,
  • refuse or customise the purposes and the advertising partners (in that case Google may show non-personalised or limited ads, which still use cookies/identifiers for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement where permitted by law).

You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy settings in the app.

Legal bases (GDPR): consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalised advertising and access to device identifiers; our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in showing non-personalised ads that keep the app free and in preventing ad fraud. Your recordings are processed only on your device, at your request.

4. How we use information

  • To record your screen (and, if you turn it on, sound) and keep your videos on your device.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.
  • To answer you if you contact us by email.

We do not use your information for automated decisions with legal effects, and we do not sell it.

5. Sharing

Your recordings are never shared by the app. Advertising data is shared with:

  • Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform) – advertising and consent management.

We may also disclose information if required by law. Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

6. Retention and deletion

Recordings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. We do not hold any data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete; if you email us, we keep the email only as long as needed to answer. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Privacy settings) without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). To exercise your rights, write to salsostaff20@gmail.com.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA where local law requires parental consent). We do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Security

Your recordings never leave your device through the app. They are stored in the app's private folder, which other apps cannot read without your action. No method of storage is completely secure, so keep your device protected with a screen lock.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published at the same address with a new "Last updated" date.
