Privacy Policy — FilmFinder Hub

Last updated: 9 October 2026

FilmFinder Hub ("the app") is published by DreamcodeStudio. Contact: salsostaff20@gmail.com.

Short version

There is no account and no server of ours. Your watchlist and settings stay on your device. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob; Premium removes them.

Movie and TV data

Most of the catalogue is built into the app. When you search for a title or person that is not in it, or open today's schedule, the app requests data from TVmaze, Wikipedia and Wikidata. These requests contain what you searched for and the usual technical data of any internet request, such as the IP address. No personal data such as your name or email is sent.

What is stored on your device

Your watchlist, recently viewed titles, settings and whether Premium is active. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store (or Google Play on Android). The payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google: we never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with the store whether Premium is active on your device.

Advertising

The free app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect the advertising identifier, approximate location derived from the IP address and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. On iPhone the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; in the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent, which you can change at any time. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Support messages

If you write to us from the Support screen, the message is sent from your own email app, and we use your email address only to reply.

What the app does not do

No account or login, no location, no contacts, no analytics or crash reporting of ours, no selling of personal data. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You can deny or revoke tracking at any time in your device settings. Under the GDPR you may ask for access to or deletion of any data we hold about you (in practice, only support emails). Write to salsostaff20@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
