Magic Themes HD – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Magic Themes HD app (Android package com.wallpaperhd.wallpaper_hd, and com.wallpaperhd.wallpaper_hd.aptoide for the Aptoide edition) collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. The app is published by DreamcodeStudio ("we", "us").

Contact: salsostaff20@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • No account, no login. We do not collect your name, email address or phone number, and we have no server of our own that receives your data.
  • Your favourites and settings stay on your device.
  • Wallpapers come from Pexels; the app sends your searches and category choices to Pexels to show pictures.
  • The app is free and shows ads: Google AdMob in the Google Play / App Store edition, start.io in the Aptoide edition. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the Google Play edition asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • We do not sell your personal information. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

2. Information we collect and how

2.1 Data kept only on your device

Your language, image-quality choice, favourite wallpapers and auto-change settings (on/off and interval) are saved in the app's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2.2 Wallpaper pictures (Pexels)

To show wallpapers, the app calls the Pexels API (api.pexels.com) and loads pictures from Pexels' servers. Pexels receives the words you search for, the category you open, and technical data that is part of every internet request, such as your IP address and device/browser information. If you turn on auto-change on Android, the app periodically downloads a random picture from Pexels in the background and sets it as your wallpaper. Pexels' privacy policy: https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/

2.3 Saving and setting wallpapers

When you tap save or set wallpaper, the picture is stored in your gallery or set as your wallpaper. The app only adds pictures; it does not read, upload or analyse your photo library.

2.4 Advertising

Google Play / App Store edition – Google AdMob. The app shows banner, native, interstitial, app-open and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and your interactions with ads, to deliver, measure and (where you allow it) personalise ads and to prevent fraud. How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Aptoide edition – start.io. The Aptoide edition shows ads from start.io (Start App Media) instead of AdMob. start.io may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions for the same purposes, under its own policy: https://www.start.io/privacy-policy/

Only one advertising partner is active in each installed edition.

2.5 Permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load wallpapers and ads.
  • Set wallpaper – to apply the wallpaper you choose (and for auto-change).
  • Storage / photos (older Android versions, and photo-library "add" access on iOS) – only to save a wallpaper you chose to save.
  • Notifications (iOS only) – optional reminders for auto-change, created on the device.
  • Advertising ID – used by the advertising partner described above.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or your existing photos.

3. Consent for ads (GDPR, EEA/UK/Switzerland)

In the Google Play / App Store edition, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show Google's consent message the first time you open it (and again when your choice needs to be renewed). Ads are not requested until this step is completed. You can accept personalised ads or refuse/customise the purposes and advertising partners; in that case Google may show non-personalised or limited ads, which may still use cookies or identifiers for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement where permitted by law.

You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app.

In the Aptoide edition, ads are served by start.io under its own privacy policy and consent mechanisms. On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in the system settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Legal bases (GDPR): consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalised advertising and access to device identifiers; our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in showing non-personalised ads to keep the app free and in preventing ad fraud; performance of the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b)) for loading the wallpapers you ask for.

4. Sharing

We do not receive or store your personal data on our own servers. Data is processed directly by:

  • Pexels – wallpaper search and images.
  • Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform) – advertising and consent management, in the Google Play / App Store edition.
  • start.io – advertising, in the Aptoide edition.

If you use "Share app", the text is passed to the app you pick in your device's share menu. These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.

5. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it (uninstall the app or clear its data). Since we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; data processed by Google, start.io or Pexels is retained according to their policies, and you can contact them directly. You can reset your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Privacy options) without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). For any question, write to salsostaff20@gmail.com.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA where local law requires parental consent). We do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Security

The app talks to Pexels and the ad networks over the internet using their secure (HTTPS) endpoints. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep data collection to the minimum the app needs.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published at the same address with a new "Last updated" date.
