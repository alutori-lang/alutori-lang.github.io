Privacy Policy — Mia Weather - Weather & Radar

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Mia Weather ("the app") is published by DreamcodeStudio. Contact: salsostaff20@gmail.com.

Short version

There is no account and no server of ours. To show the weather for where you are, the app uses your location, if you allow it. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your device location to show the forecast, radar and alerts for where you are. The coordinates are sent only to the weather services listed below to get that forecast; they are not stored by us. You can deny location and search for a city instead.

Weather and map services

To show forecasts, air quality, marine data, maps and radar the app requests data from Open-Meteo, RainViewer, OpenStreetMap tiles, Blitzortung (lightning) and Windy. These requests contain the place you are looking at (coordinates or map area) and the usual technical data of any internet request, such as the IP address. No personal data such as your name or email is sent.

What is stored on your device

Your saved places, settings and the last forecast (for offline use and the home screen widget). Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts, the app shows local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to a server of ours.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect the advertising identifier, approximate location derived from the IP address and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. On iPhone the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; in the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent, which you can change at any time. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What the app does not do

No account or login, no contacts, no selling of personal data. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You can deny or revoke location permission and tracking at any time in your device settings. For any privacy request write to salsostaff20@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
