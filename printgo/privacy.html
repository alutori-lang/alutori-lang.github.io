Privacy Policy — PrintGo: Design & Print

Last updated: 9 October 2026

PrintGo ("the app") is published by DreamcodeStudio. Contact: salsostaff20@gmail.com.

Short version

There is no account and no server of ours. Your designs, logos and settings stay on your device. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob; PRO removes them.

Your designs and photos

Designs, logos and the photos you place in them are stored only on your device. If you allow it, the app reads the photos you choose from your library and saves finished designs into it. Background removal runs on the device: your photos are never uploaded.

Online features

When you search the free photo banks, your search words are sent to Pexels, Unsplash or Pixabay to show results. When you pick a font, it is downloaded from Google Fonts. These requests contain only what is needed (search words, font name) and the usual technical data of any internet request, such as the IP address. Everything else works offline.

Purchases

PRO is bought through the App Store (or Google Play on Android). The payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google: we never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with the store whether PRO is active on your device.

Advertising

The free app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect the advertising identifier, approximate location derived from the IP address and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. On iPhone the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; in the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent, which you can change at any time. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What the app does not do

No account or login, no location, no contacts, no analytics or crash reporting of ours, no selling of personal data. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You can deny or revoke photo access and tracking at any time in your device settings. Under the GDPR you may ask for access to or deletion of any data we hold about you (in practice, only emails you send us). Write to salsostaff20@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
