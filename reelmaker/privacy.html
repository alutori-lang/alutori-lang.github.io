Privacy Policy — Reel Maker

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Reel Maker ("the app") is published by DreamcodeStudio. Contact: alutori@gmail.com.

Short version

There is no account and no server of ours. The photos, videos and music you use stay on your device: the app edits and renders your videos on the device itself. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses the data described below.

Your photos, videos and music

The app opens only the photos, videos and songs you pick. They are processed on your device to create your video and are never uploaded by the app. A video leaves the device only when you choose to share it with another app.

What the app stores on the device

Your projects, settings and whether Premium is active. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect the device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. On iPhone the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; in the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, which you can change at any time from the app's settings. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Premium is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled by the store; the app only receives confirmation of what you bought, to unlock Premium.

What the app does not do

No account or login, no contacts, no precise location, no analytics of ours, no selling of personal data. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can refuse tracking, change ad consent in the app's settings, reset the advertising identifier in your device settings, or buy Premium to remove ads. For any request write to alutori@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
