Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a document?

Open the app, tap Scan now and point the camera at your document. Scan King automatically detects the edges and straightens the page. You can add more pages, then export or share a PDF.

The scanner won't open / camera permission

Scan King needs camera access. Go to Settings → Scan King → Camera and make sure it's enabled, then reopen the app.

How do I export or share a PDF?

Open a document and tap Share to export a PDF, or the printer icon to print. In Settings you can choose the export quality (up to 600 DPI with Premium).

What does Scan King Premium include?

Premium removes ads, removes the watermark from your PDFs and unlocks the maximum 600 DPI export quality. It's available as a monthly or yearly subscription with a 3-day free trial.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. From there you can change or cancel at any time. If you reinstall the app or switch device, tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen.

Where are my documents stored?

Everything stays locally on your device. Your scans and PDFs are never uploaded to any server — they're shared only when you choose to share them. See our Privacy Policy.
