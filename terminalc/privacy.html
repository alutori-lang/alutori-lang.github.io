Privacy Policy — TerminalC

Last updated: 9 October 2026

TerminalC ("the app") is published by DreamcodeStudio. Contact: salsostaff20@gmail.com.

Short version

There is no account and no server of ours. Your files, commands and history stay on your phone. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob; Premium removes them.

Your files and commands

The terminal works on the app's own storage and on the folders you choose to add as drives. Files, command history, saved SSH hosts and settings are stored only on your device; SSH passwords and keys are kept in the system's secure storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network commands

Commands such as ping, nslookup, tracert, curl, ftp, telnet, whois, speedtest and the SSH/SFTP connection talk directly to the address or server you type, as any terminal would. We do not see or store that traffic.

The ai command

If you use the ai command, your question is sent to Anthropic (Claude) with the API key you enter yourself, and Anthropic's privacy policy applies to it. Nothing is sent if you do not use the command.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the app store of your phone. The payment is handled entirely by the store: we never see your card or payment details.

Advertising

The free app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect the advertising identifier, approximate location derived from the IP address and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. On iPhone the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking; in the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent, which you can change at any time in Settings. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What the app does not do

No account or login, no location, no contacts, no analytics or crash reporting of ours, no selling of personal data. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to or deletion of any data we hold about you (in practice, only emails you send us). Write to salsostaff20@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
