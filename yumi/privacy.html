Privacy Policy — YUMI: Learn & Play for Kids

Last updated: 30 September 2026

YUMI: Learn & Play for Kids ("the app") is an educational app for children aged 3 to 7, published by DreamcodeStudio. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does and does not do with information.

Short version

The app collects no personal data. There is no account, no server of ours, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing your child says or does is sent to us or to anyone else: everything stays on the phone or tablet.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves only these things, and only in the device's own app storage:

  • the stars earned and the number of lessons, stories and games completed;
  • the age band chosen by the parent (3-4, 5-6, 7);
  • the chosen language and the voice settings;
  • how much of the free daily allowance has been used, today's date and the last moment the app was opened, so that the allowance starts again at midnight and a changed clock cannot refill it by mistake;
  • whether a Yumi Premium subscription or the one-off purchase is active, and which one.

This information never leaves the device. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled, and a parent can erase it at any time from Parents area → Reset progress.

Microphone and speech

Your child can answer and ask questions out loud. When the microphone button is pressed, the device's own speech recognition service turns the spoken words into text. The app itself never records, stores or transmits any audio: it receives only the recognised words, uses them on the device and then discards them. The microphone is only active while the child is speaking, and the app asks for the microphone permission before the first use.

Where the recognition happens depends on the device. The app asks for on-device (offline) recognition every time the device and the chosen language support it. When that is not available, the device's own speech service may send the audio to the company that provides that service (the maker of the device's operating system) to turn it into words; that processing is governed by that provider's privacy policy. The app never sees or keeps the audio in either case. Parents area → Privacy and safety states which of the two is happening on that particular device, in that language.

"Ask Yumi"

In "Ask Yumi" the child may ask Yumi a question out loud. Nothing the child says ever leaves the device:

  • calculations (for example "how much is 5 times 3") are worked out by the app itself;
  • every other question is answered from a library of several hundred children's questions and answers that is written in advance and built into the app, in each of the app's ten languages. The recognised words are compared with that library on the device, and a ready-made answer is read aloud;
  • when Yumi does not know an answer, Yumi says so kindly, suggests asking a grown-up and offers a fun fact or a game;
  • questions about subjects that are not for small children, or about personal details such as an address or a password, get a kind sentence suggesting to ask a parent.

"Ask Yumi" makes no network call of any kind, uses no artificial intelligence service and does not keep the questions after answering them.

Subscriptions and purchases

The app offers an optional subscription, Yumi Premium, and a one-off purchase. They are handled entirely by the app store the app was downloaded from: the app never sees, asks for or stores a name, an address, a card number or any other payment detail. The store processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy, and the purchase belongs to the store account of the adult who bought it, not to the child.

All the app keeps on the device is a yes/no flag saying that a purchase is active and which plan it is. At every start the app asks the store whether the purchase is still valid, so a cancelled subscription stops unlocking the app on its own. Nothing about the purchase is sent anywhere else.

Every price, plan, buy button and external link lives behind the parents lock: a child cannot see a price or start a purchase.

Downloads the app may make

These downloads never carry anything about the child:

  • Yumi's voice. For some languages the app can download, once, a free open-source voice file (about 21 MB) from a public file server, so that Yumi can speak with a cartoon voice even offline. It is a plain request for a public file: no identifier, no account and no information about the child or the device is sent with it.
  • New content. The app is able to download new stories and lesson packs from a single public address set by the app owner. No address is configured at the moment, so this download never happens; the subscription is not sold on the promise of new content. If it is ever switched on, it will be a plain request for a public file, with nothing about the child or the device sent with it.

No advertising, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers, no analytics and no tracking tools of any kind. Nothing is shared with third parties for marketing, and no profile of the child is ever built.

Children and parents

The app is designed for families. The child's part of the app contains no external links. Settings, statistics, the subscription page and the privacy statement all live in a parents area protected by a multiplication question that a small child cannot answer. The only links that leave the app — this policy, the terms of use and the store's subscription page — are inside that area.

Children's privacy laws

Because no personal data is collected, used or disclosed, the app does not collect information from children under 13 (COPPA) or from any user in the sense of the GDPR. If you believe any information has reached us by mistake, write to the address below and it will be deleted.
